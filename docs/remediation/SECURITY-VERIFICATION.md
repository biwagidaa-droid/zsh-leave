## Gate decision

- **G1 Authorization and scope: PASS** for tested API/service surfaces, including expired manager and HR union cases.
- **G2 Confidentiality: PASS** for sensitive balance, report, notification, Bradford and export routes represented by regression tests.
- **G3 Employment lifecycle: PASS** for inclusive dates, expired manager service calls, and session rejection on next request. Proactive session revocation is absent.
- **G4 Account activation: PASS** for token security and single-use/rate limits.
- **G6 Audit integrity: NOT VERIFIED** because `TRUNCATE` was reproduced and role privileges are unknown. Raw INSERT is also not controlled by the triggers.
- **G9 Attachments: NOT VERIFIED** at infrastructure level; application controls are tested, but production storage/scanner are absent.

Full exact commands and observed counts are catalogued in `BACKEND-FINAL-AUDIT.md`; PostgreSQL-specific evidence is in `POSTGRESQL-EVIDENCE.md`.
